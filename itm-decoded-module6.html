📧 First Due Sends Notices

First Due sends email notifications at 90, 60, and 30 days before go-live.

📬 U.S. Mail: Physical mail notices are also sent at 60 and 30 days as needed to ensure maximum reach.

AHJ reviews and approves all messaging before it goes out.

💡 Why Multiple Notices & Channels?

Contractors are busy. A single email gets lost. Three email touches over 90 days—plus U.S. Mail at 60 and 30 days—ensures the message gets through. First Due handles all the outreach and tracks adoption at each stage so we can adjust strategy if needed.

Section 5: Keys to Success

What Makes Implementations Successful

After many implementations, we've identified the key factors that determine success:

📣

Clear Communication

AHJs who clearly communicate the go-live date and expectations see highest adoption.

🏛️

Leadership Buy-In

When the Fire Marshal visibly supports the program, contractors take it seriously.

📅

Firm Timeline

Setting—and sticking to—a go-live date creates urgency for adoption.

⚠️ What Slows Implementations

  • Delayed review of service provider list
  • Unclear or conflicting go-live messaging
  • Allowing indefinite transition periods
  • Not participating in check-in calls

✅ What Accelerates Success

  • Quick review/approval of SP list
  • Fire Marshal participates in webinar
  • Clear "digital only" enforcement date
  • Active engagement in check-in calls
